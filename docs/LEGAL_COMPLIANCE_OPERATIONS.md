# Legal and Compliance Operations

This project can track legal/compliance readiness, but it must not claim SOC2, ISO, legal approval, regulatory compliance, or insurance coverage unless real evidence exists.

Track:

- privacy policy owner and review date
- terms of service owner and review date
- data processing / DPA status
- vulnerability disclosure policy
- incident notification policy
- payment/refund policy
- jurisdiction notes
- legal counsel reference when available

Safe public language:

```text
Compliance evidence is being tracked internally. No external certification is claimed unless explicitly shown with a real certificate/report.
```

API:

```text
GET  /api/v1/security-os/trust-operations/legal-compliance
POST /api/v1/security-os/trust-operations/legal-compliance
```
