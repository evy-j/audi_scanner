# Customer Pilot Program

Customer pilots must represent real organizations/projects only. Do not add fake customer names, fake logos, fake usage claims, or fake references.

Required evidence:

- organization/project identifier
- pilot scope and success criteria
- support owner
- consent state: private, internal-only, public-reference allowed, or revoked
- feedback notes and dates

Public use rules:

- no public logo without consent
- no case study without approval
- no revenue/user count claim without source evidence

API:

```text
GET  /api/v1/security-os/trust-operations/customer-pilots
POST /api/v1/security-os/trust-operations/customer-pilots
```
